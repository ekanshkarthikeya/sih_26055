\begin{abstract}
Traditional electronic support (ES) receivers rely on deterministic round-robin scanning routines, yielding low intercept probabilities ($P_{\text{int}} \approx 4\%$) against modern agile, low duty-cycle emitters. We formulate wideband local oscillator tuning as a Restless Multi-Armed Bandit (RMAB) under partial observability. By combining online Beta-Bernoulli conjugate belief updating, hardware slewing cost regularized Whittle indexation, and real-time Sequential Difference Histogramming (SDIF), our system attains $70.53\%$ mean $P_{\text{int}}$ across the official Alan Turing Synthetic Radar Benchmark—a $16.4\times$ multiplier over legacy architectures—with guaranteed worst-case starvation bounds ($\le 60\text{ ms}$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematical Formulation}
Let $K = 16$ denote the instantaneous sub-bands. At decision epoch $t$, the receiver selects channel $a(t) \in \{0, \dots, K-1\}$. The state of each arm follows an unobserved Markov jump process $S_k(t) \in \{0, 1\}$.

\subsection{Conjugate Bayesian Belief Updating}
We track arm occupancy using conjugate Beta distributions:
\begin{equation}
b_k(t) = \mathbb{E}[\theta_k \mid \alpha_k, \beta_k] = \frac{\alpha_k}{\alpha_k + \beta_k}
\end{equation}
Upon dwelling on band $k$ with pulse count $h$:
\begin{align}
\alpha_k &\leftarrow \alpha_k + \min(h, 10), \quad \text{if } h > 0 \\
\beta_k &\leftarrow \beta_k + 2.0, \quad \text{if } h = 0 \text{ (Step-on-Miss)}
\end{align}
Unvisited channels experience Markovian information aging: $\alpha_j \leftarrow \max(1.0, 0.995 \alpha_j)$, $\beta_j \leftarrow \max(1.0, 0.995 \beta_j)$.

\subsection{Hardware-Regularized Whittle Index}
Frequency transitions incur synthesizer settling times $t_{\text{settle}} \propto \log_2(1 + |\Delta k|)$. The decision index is formulated as:
\begin{equation}
I_k(t) = \underbrace{(b_k(t) + \mathbf{T}_{a(t-1), k}) \cdot \Phi_k(t) \cdot w_k}_{\text{Marginal Interception Gain}} + \underbrace{\lambda \left(\frac{t - \tau_k}{\tau_{\max}}\right)}_{\text{Strict Anti-Starvation}} - \underbrace{\kappa \log_2(1 + |k - a(t-1)|)}_{\text{PLL Slew Penalty}}
\end{equation}
where $\Phi_k(t)$ is the temporal burst phase projector, and $\mathbf{T}_{i,j}$ is the online cross-band transition matrix capturing agile frequency hopping.
